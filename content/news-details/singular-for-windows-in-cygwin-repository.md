<p><strong>Singular</strong> for Windows is now available as part 	  of the official Cygwin repository. You can benefit from this fact due to easy setup routines, seamless integration in your existing Cygwin environment and a world-wide network of mirrors. All versions of <strong>Singular</strong> and all setup routines are powered by <a href="http://cygwin.com/">Cygwin</a> and their setup program. We wish to thank the developers of setup.exe and the maintainers of the Cygwin packages.</p>
