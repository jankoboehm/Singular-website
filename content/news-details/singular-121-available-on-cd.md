<p><strong>Singular</strong> version 1.2.1 is now also available on CD. Our entire ftp and WWW site, as well as ready-to-run Singular executables for various platforms are contained on this CD. 100 copies of it were freely distributed to the attendees of our presentation at ICM'98. Unfortunately, we have none left and are unable to distribute further Singular CD's (at least for the time being).</p>
