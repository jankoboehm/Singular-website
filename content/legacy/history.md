<td align="right" valign="top"><strong>1984 </strong></td>
<td align="left" valign="top">Greuel/Pfister: Do there exist complete intersection singularities   which are not quasi-homogeneous but whose Poincare-complex is exact?</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td align="right" valign="top"><strong> 1987 </strong></td>
<td align="left" valign="top">Pfister et al (Humboldt Uni Berlin): Developed package <span style="font-weight:bold; font-variant: small-caps;">Buchmora</span> for Atari in   Modula-2 to verify existence.<br /></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td align="right" valign="top"><strong> 1989 </strong></td>
<td align="left" valign="top"><span style="font-weight:bold; font-variant: small-caps;">Buchmora</span> is renamed to Singular. Jointly developed by groups from Berlin (Pfister) and Kaiserslautern (Greuel).</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td align="right" valign="top"><strong> 1990 </strong></td>
<td align="left" valign="top">Ported to Unix; First user manual released.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td align="right" valign="top"><strong> 1993 </strong></td>
<td align="left" valign="top">Rewritten in C; Singular programming language - Libraries established.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td align="right" valign="top"><strong> 1996 </strong></td>
<td align="left" valign="top">Multivariate polynomial factorization and gcd implemented.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td align="right" valign="top"><strong> 1997 </strong></td>
<td align="left" valign="top">Singular release 1.0 (with multivariate polynomial factorization,   gcd, syzygies, resolutions, and communication links).</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td align="right" valign="top"><strong> 1998 </strong></td>
<td align="left" valign="top">Singular release 1.2 (faster with primary decomposition and normalization).</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td align="right" valign="top"><strong> 1999 </strong></td>
<td align="left" valign="top">Singular release 1.4   (even faster, with numerical data types and algorithms,   monodromy, moduli of space curves, and debugger).</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td align="right" valign="top"><strong> 2001 </strong></td>
<td align="left" valign="top">Singular release 2.0</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td align="right" valign="top"><strong> 2002 </strong></td>
<td align="left" valign="top">Textbook  <a href="../publications/97.html" target="_self">"A 	  Singular Introduction to Commutative Algebra"</a> published by Springer Verlag (including a CD containing a 	distribution of Singular version 2.0.3).</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td align="right" valign="top"><strong> 2004 </strong></td>
<td align="left" valign="top">The Singular team was awarded the <a href="../component/content/article/13687.html">Richard D. Jenks Memorial Prize</a> for Excellence in 	Software Engineering for Computer Algebra.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td align="right" valign="top"><strong> 2005 </strong></td>
<td align="left" valign="top">Textbook <a href="/index.php/component/content/100.html">"Computing in Algebraic Geometry: A Quick Start using Singular"</a> published by Springer Verlag</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td align="right" valign="top"><strong> 2005 </strong></td>
<td align="left" valign="top">Singular release 3.0 (with dynamic modules, name spaces, noncommutative 	computations, resolution of singularities, absolute 	factorization, etc.)</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td align="right" valign="top"><strong> 2007 </strong></td>
<td align="left" valign="top">Second edition of the textbook  <a href="../publications/97.html" target="_self">"A 	  Singular Introduction to Commutative Algebra"</a> published by Springer Verlag (including a CD containing a 	distribution of Singular version 3.0.3).</td>
</tr>
